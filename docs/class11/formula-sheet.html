Class XI — Formula Sheet

Every boxed formula from every chapter, in one place. Print this page or skim it the night before the exam.

2. Units and Measurements

📐 Combining errors
Addition/Subtraction → absolute errors add: Δ(A ± B) = ΔA + ΔB
Multiplication/Division → relative errors add: Δ(AB)/AB = ΔA/A + ΔB/B
📐 Dimensional formulae to remember
Velocity → [M⁰L¹T⁻¹]
Acceleration → [M⁰L¹T⁻²]
Force → [M¹L¹T⁻²]
Work / Energy → [M¹L²T⁻²]
Power → [M¹L²T⁻³]
Pressure → [M¹L⁻¹T⁻²]

3. Motion in a Straight Line

📐 The three kinematics equations (constant a)
v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as

4. Motion in a Plane

📐 Resolving a vector into components
Ax = A cos θ (x-component)
Ay = A sin θ (y-component)
A = √(Ax² + Ay²) , θ = tan⁻¹(Ay/Ax)
📐 Key projectile results (launch angle θ, speed u)
Time of flight: T = 2u sinθ / g
Maximum height: H = u² sin²θ / 2g
Range: R = u² sin2θ / g
Range is maximum when θ = 45°
📐 Circular motion
Centripetal acceleration: a = v²/r = ω²r
ω (angular velocity) = v/r = 2π/T

5. Laws of Motion

📐 Newton's second law (general form)
F = dp/dt
For constant mass: F = ma
📐 Friction
f ≤ μsN (static)
f = μkN (kinetic)
μk < μs — always easier to keep something moving than to start it
📐 Maximum safe speed on a banked curve (angle θ, friction μ)
v²max = rg(tanθ + μ)/(1 − μ tanθ)

6. Work, Energy and Power

📐 Work–Energy theorem
Wnet = ΔKE = ½mv² − ½mu²
Kinetic Energy: KE = ½mv²
📐 Common potential energies
Gravitational PE: U = mgh
Spring PE: U = ½kx²
📐 Power
Power = Work done / time = W/t
Instantaneous power: P = F·v

7. System of Particles and Rotational Motion

📐 Centre of mass of a two-particle system
xcm = (m1x1 + m2x2) / (m1 + m2)
📐 Rotational quantities
Torque: τ = r × F (τ = rF sinθ)
Angular momentum: L = r × p = Iω
Newton's 2nd law for rotation: τ = dL/dt
📐 Moment of inertia & radius of gyration
I = Σmr² = MK²
K = radius of gyration

8. Gravitation

📐 Newton's law of gravitation
F = G m1m2 / r²
G = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N m² kg⁻²
📐 Variation of g
With altitude h: g' = g(1 − 2h/R) (h ≪ R)
With depth d: g' = g(1 − d/R)
📐 Energy & speeds
Gravitational PE: U = −GMm/r
Escape speed: ve = √(2GM/R)
Orbital speed (near surface): vo = √(GM/R) = ve/√2

9. Mechanical Properties of Solids

📐 Types of modulus of elasticity
Young's modulus: Y = (F/A)/(ΔL/L) — for length change
Bulk modulus: K = −ΔP/(ΔV/V) — for volume change
Shear (rigidity) modulus: G = shear stress/shear strain
📐 Elastic potential energy stored in a stretched wire
U = ½ × stress × strain × volume = ½ × F × ΔL

10. Mechanical Properties of Fluids

📐 Fluid pressure
P = F/A
Pressure due to fluid column: P = hρg
📐 Bernoulli's theorem (along a streamline, no viscosity)
P + ½ρv² + ρgh = constant

11. Thermal Properties of Matter

📐 Thermal expansion
Linear: L = L0(1 + αΔT)
Area: A = A0(1 + βΔT), β ≈ 2α
Volume: V = V0(1 + γΔT), γ ≈ 3α
📐 Heat, specific heat & latent heat
Q = mcΔT (c = specific heat capacity)
Q = mL (L = latent heat, during change of state — temperature doesn't change!)
📐 Stefan's law & Wien's law
Stefan's law: E = σT⁴ (energy radiated per unit area per unit time)
Wien's displacement law: λm T = constant

12. Thermodynamics

📐 First law of thermodynamics
ΔQ = ΔU + ΔW
(Heat supplied = change in internal energy + work done by the system)
📐 Work done in special processes
Isothermal: W = nRT ln(V2/V1)
Adiabatic: PVᵞ = constant (γ = Cp/Cv)
Cyclic process: Wnet = area enclosed by the P-V loop

13. Kinetic Theory

📐 Ideal gas equation
PV = nRT
R = universal gas constant = 8.31 J mol⁻¹K⁻¹
📐 Kinetic theory results
Average KE per molecule: (3/2)kT (k = Boltzmann constant)
rms speed: vrms = √(3RT/M)

14. Oscillations

📐 SHM equations
F = −kx (restoring force)
x(t) = A sin(ωt + φ)
ω = 2π/T = 2πf
📐 Spring oscillator
T = 2π√(m/k)
Energy in SHM: E = ½kA² (total, constant throughout motion)
📐 Simple pendulum (small angle θ)
T = 2π√(L/g)

15. Waves

📐 Wave basics
v = f λ (speed = frequency × wavelength)
y(x,t) = A sin(kx − ωt) — a progressive wave
📐 Strings & organ pipes (fundamental + harmonics)
Stretched string, both ends fixed: fn = n v / 2L (n = 1,2,3…)
Closed organ pipe: only odd harmonics
Open organ pipe: all harmonics
📐 Beat frequency
fbeat = |f1 − f2|
