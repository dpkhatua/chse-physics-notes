Kinetic Theory

Gas pressure explained by billions of tiny, chaotic collisions.

1 Equation of State & Kinetic Theory Assumptions

📐 Ideal gas equation
PV = nRT
R = universal gas constant = 8.31 J mol⁻¹K⁻¹
🖊️ Kinetic theory pictures a gas as tiny particles in constant, random motion, colliding elastically.
  • Gas molecules are point masses in continuous random motion.
  • Collisions between molecules (and with walls) are perfectly elastic.
  • No intermolecular forces except during collision (ideal gas assumption).
  • Pressure arises from the rate of momentum transfer to the walls.

2 Kinetic Interpretation of Temperature & rms Speed

🧮 Derivation: Pressure of an ideal gas (kinetic theory)
Given / Setup: N molecules of mass m each, in a cubical box of side L, moving randomly; consider one molecule moving with velocity component vx hitting a wall.
  1. Each collision with the wall reverses vx, so change in momentum per collision = 2mvx.
  2. Time between successive collisions with the same wall = 2L/vx, so force from one molecule = 2mvx / (2L/vx) = mvx²/L.
  3. Summing over all N molecules and averaging, then extending to 3 dimensions (vx²=vy²=vz²=⟨v²⟩/3), total pressure P = (1/3)(Nm/V)⟨v²⟩ = (1/3)ρ⟨v²⟩
✅ P = (1/3) ρ ⟨v²⟩ , i.e. PV = (1/3)Nm⟨v²⟩
📐 Kinetic theory results
Average KE per molecule: (3/2)kT (k = Boltzmann constant)
rms speed: vrms = √(3RT/M)
✏️ Solved Example
Q. Find the rms speed of oxygen molecules at 27°C. (M = 32×10⁻³ kg/mol, R = 8.31 J/mol·K)
  1. T = 27 + 273 = 300 K
  2. vrms = √(3RT/M) = √(3×8.31×300/32×10⁻³) = √(7479/0.032) = √(233719)
🎯 Answer: vrms ≈ 483 m/s

Temperature is simply a measure of the average kinetic energy of gas molecules — this is kinetic theory's biggest insight.

3 Degrees of Freedom & Specific Heats

Degrees of freedom = independent ways a molecule can store energy (translation, rotation, vibration). By the law of equipartition of energy, each degree of freedom gets (1/2)kT of energy on average.

Quick countsMonatomic gas: f = 3 (only translation). Diatomic gas: f = 5 (3 translation + 2 rotation, at moderate temperatures).

⚡ Quick Recap

  • PV = nRT connects pressure, volume, moles and temperature for an ideal gas.
  • Kinetic theory: pressure comes from molecular collisions; temperature ∝ average KE.
  • vrms = √(3RT/M); rms speed increases with T, decreases with molar mass.
  • Equipartition: each degree of freedom contributes (1/2)kT of average energy.
