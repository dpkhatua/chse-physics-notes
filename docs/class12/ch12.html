Atoms

From a solid pudding of charge to a tiny nucleus with electrons orbiting like planets.

1 Rutherford's Alpha-Scattering Experiment

Firing alpha particles at thin gold foil, Rutherford found most passed straight through, but a few bounced back sharply — proving atoms are mostly empty space with a tiny, dense, positively charged nucleus at the centre, around which electrons revolve.

2 Bohr's Model of the Hydrogen Atom

🖊️ Bohr's model: electrons occupy only certain fixed, quantised circular orbits.
🧮 Derivation: Radius and energy of the nth Bohr orbit
Given / Setup: An electron of charge −e revolves around a nucleus of charge +e in a circular orbit of radius rn, with quantised angular momentum.
  1. Coulomb force provides centripetal force: ke²/rn² = mv²/rn
  2. Bohr's quantisation condition: mvrn = nh/2π → v = nh/2πmrn
  3. Substituting v into the force equation and solving for rn: rn = n²h²ε0/(πme²) = n² × 0.529 Å
  4. Total energy En = KE + PE = ½mv² − ke²/rn; substituting rn gives En = −13.6/n² eV
✅ rn = n² × 0.529 Å, En = −13.6/n² eV
📐 Bohr's postulates & key results
Angular momentum is quantised: mvr = nh/2π
Radius of nth orbit: rn = n² × 0.529 Å (for hydrogen)
Energy of nth orbit: En = −13.6/n² eV
✏️ Solved Example
Q. Find the energy of an electron in the 2nd Bohr orbit of hydrogen, and the energy released when it jumps to the 1st orbit.
  1. E2 = −13.6/2² = −3.4 eV
  2. E1 = −13.6/1² = −13.6 eV
  3. Energy released = E2 − E1 = −3.4 − (−13.6)
🎯 Answer: Energy released = 10.2 eV (emitted as a photon)

Electrons jumping between these fixed orbits absorb or emit photons of specific energies — explaining the sharp, discrete hydrogen line spectrum instead of a continuous glow.

⚡ Quick Recap

  • Rutherford: atom = tiny dense positive nucleus + mostly empty space + orbiting electrons.
  • Bohr: angular momentum quantised (mvr = nh/2π); En = −13.6/n² eV for hydrogen.
  • Electron transitions between fixed orbits → discrete emission/absorption lines.
