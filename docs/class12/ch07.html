Alternating Current

Current that keeps changing its mind about direction, 50 times a second in India.

1 AC Basics & rms Values

🖊️ Voltage and current in an AC circuit can be out of phase depending on the circuit elements.
📐 AC fundamentals
v(t) = v0 sinωt , i(t) = i0 sin(ωt − φ)
rms value: vrms = v0/√2 (what your multimeter reads!)
✏️ Solved Example
Q. The peak voltage of the AC mains in India is about 311 V. Find its rms value.
  1. vrms = v0/√2 = 311/1.414
🎯 Answer: vrms ≈ 220 V

2 Reactance, Impedance & Series LCR Resonance

🧮 Derivation: Impedance of a series LCR circuit
Given / Setup: A resistor R, inductor L and capacitor C in series, carrying current i(t) = i0 sinωt.
  1. Voltage across R is in phase with current: VR = i0R
  2. Voltage across L leads current by 90°: VL = i0XL (XL = ωL); voltage across C lags current by 90°: VC = i0XC (XC = 1/ωC)
  3. Using a phasor diagram, VL and VC are opposite (180° apart), so net reactive voltage = i0(XL − XC)
  4. Resultant voltage (phasor sum of VR and net reactive voltage): V0 = i0√(R² + (XL−XC)²)
✅ Z = V0/i0 = √(R² + (XL − XC)²)
📐 Reactance & impedance in a series LCR circuit
Inductive reactance: XL = ωL
Capacitive reactance: XC = 1/ωC
Impedance: Z = √(R² + (XL − XC)²)
Resonance (Z minimum, current maximum) at: ω0 = 1/√(LC)
📐 Power in AC circuits
Average power: P = vrms irms cosφ (cosφ = power factor)
✏️ Solved Example
Q. A series LCR circuit has R=30Ω, XL=50Ω, XC=10Ω. Find the impedance and the phase angle.
  1. Z = √(R² + (XL−XC)²) = √(30² + 40²) = √(900+1600) = √2500
  2. tanφ = (XL−XC)/R = 40/30 = 1.33
🎯 Answer: Z = 50 Ω, φ ≈ 53° (voltage leads current, since XL > XC)
Wattless currentIn a purely inductive or purely capacitive AC circuit, average power consumed is zero even though current flows — this is called 'wattless current' (cosφ = 0, since φ = 90°).

3 AC Generator & Transformer

🖊️ A transformer changes AC voltage using two coils linked by a common iron core.

An AC generator converts mechanical energy to electrical energy using EM induction — a coil rotates in a magnetic field, and flux through it changes sinusoidally, inducing a sinusoidal EMF.

📐 Ideal transformer relation
Vs/Vp = Ns/Np = Ip/Is
✏️ Solved Example
Q. A step-down transformer converts 220V to 11V. If the primary coil has 2000 turns, find the number of secondary turns.
  1. Ns/Np = Vs/Vp → Ns = Np × Vs/Vp = 2000 × 11/220
🎯 Answer: Ns = 100 turns

⚡ Quick Recap

  • rms value = peak/√2; this is what AC meters actually display.
  • Series LCR: Z = √(R²+(XL−XC)²); resonance at ω0 = 1/√(LC), where Z is minimum.
  • Average AC power = vrmsirmscosφ; wattless current occurs when φ = 90°.
  • Transformer: Vs/Vp = Ns/Np — steps voltage up or down using mutual induction.
