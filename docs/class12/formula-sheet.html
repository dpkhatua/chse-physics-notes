Class XII — Formula Sheet

Every boxed formula from every chapter, in one place. Print this page or skim it the night before the exam.

1. Electric Charges and Fields

📐 Coulomb's law
F = k q1q2 / r² , k = 1/(4πε0) ≈ 9 × 10⁹ N m² C⁻²
📐 Electric field
E = F/q = kQ/r² (due to a point charge)
📐 Electric dipole
Dipole moment: p = q × 2a (from −q to +q)
Axial field: Eaxial = 2kpr/(r²−a²)² ≈ 2kp/r³
Torque in uniform field: τ = p × E
📐 Gauss's law
Φ = ∮E·dA = qenclosed / ε0

2. Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance

📐 Potential
V = kQ/r (point charge)
Potential energy of two charges: U = kq1q2/r
📐 Capacitance
C = Q/V
Parallel plate capacitor: C = ε0A/d (vacuum), C = Kε0A/d (with dielectric of constant K)
Energy stored: U = ½CV² = ½QV = Q²/2C
📐 Series & parallel combination
Series: 1/Ceq = 1/C1 + 1/C2 + … (charge same on each, voltage adds)
Parallel: Ceq = C1 + C2 + … (voltage same on each, charge adds)

3. Current Electricity

📐 Ohm's law & resistivity
V = IR (Ohm's law, for ohmic conductors)
R = ρL/A (ρ = resistivity, a material property)
I = neAvd (n = free electron density)
📐 Cell equations
EMF ε = V + Ir (r = internal resistance)
Terminal voltage: V = ε − Ir
📐 Kirchhoff's rules
Junction rule (charge conservation): ΣIin = ΣIout at any junction
Loop rule (energy conservation): ΣV = 0 around any closed loop

4. Moving Charges and Magnetism

📐 Field due to currents
Biot–Savart law: dB = (μ0/4π) · I dl × r̂ / r²
Field at centre of circular loop: B = μ0I / 2R
Long straight wire: B = μ0I / 2πr
Ideal solenoid (inside): B = μ0nI (n = turns per unit length)
📐 Magnetic forces
Force on a moving charge: F = qv × B (magnitude qvB sinθ)
Force on current-carrying wire: F = IL × B
Force between two parallel wires: F/L = μ0I1I2 / 2πd
📐 Current loop as a magnetic dipole
Magnetic moment: m = IA
Torque: τ = m × B

5. Magnetism and Matter

📐 Magnetic dipole (bar magnet)
Magnetic dipole moment: m = qm × 2l
Torque in uniform field: τ = m × B

6. Electromagnetic Induction

📐 Faraday's law of EM induction
ε = −dΦ/dt (Φ = magnetic flux = B·A cosθ)
📐 Induced EMF from inductance
ε = −L(dI/dt) (self)
ε2 = −M(dI1/dt) (mutual)

7. Alternating Current

📐 AC fundamentals
v(t) = v0 sinωt , i(t) = i0 sin(ωt − φ)
rms value: vrms = v0/√2 (what your multimeter reads!)
📐 Reactance & impedance in a series LCR circuit
Inductive reactance: XL = ωL
Capacitive reactance: XC = 1/ωC
Impedance: Z = √(R² + (XL − XC)²)
Resonance (Z minimum, current maximum) at: ω0 = 1/√(LC)
📐 Power in AC circuits
Average power: P = vrms irms cosφ (cosφ = power factor)
📐 Ideal transformer relation
Vs/Vp = Ns/Np = Ip/Is

8. Electromagnetic Waves

📐 Displacement current
Id = ε0 (dΦE/dt)
📐 Speed of EM waves
c = 1/√(μ0ε0) ≈ 3 × 10⁸ m/s = f λ

9. Ray Optics and Optical Instruments

📐 Mirror & refraction formulas
Mirror formula: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f
Snell's law: n1 sinθ1 = n2 sinθ2
📐 Lens formulas
Thin lens formula: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f
Lens maker's formula: 1/f = (n−1)(1/R1 − 1/R2)
Power of a lens: P = 1/f (f in metres, P in dioptre)
Combination of thin lenses in contact: 1/F = 1/f1 + 1/f2 + …
📐 Magnifying power of astronomical telescope (normal adjustment)
M = fo/fe (fo, fe = focal lengths of objective, eyepiece)

10. Wave Optics

📐 Interference fringe width
β = λD / d (D = slit-to-screen distance, d = slit separation)

11. Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter

📐 Einstein's photoelectric equation
hν = φ0 + KEmax
KEmax = hν − hν0 (ν0 = threshold frequency)
📐 de Broglie wavelength
λ = h/p = h/mv

12. Atoms

📐 Bohr's postulates & key results
Angular momentum is quantised: mvr = nh/2π
Radius of nth orbit: rn = n² × 0.529 Å (for hydrogen)
Energy of nth orbit: En = −13.6/n² eV

13. Nuclei

📐 Nuclear size
R = R0 A^(1/3) , R0 ≈ 1.2 fm
📐 Mass-energy relation
E = mc²
Mass defect: Δm = (Zmp + Nmn) − Mnucleus
Binding energy: BE = Δm c²
